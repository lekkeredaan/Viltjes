VILTJES – overige cartoons, gratis te maken

Zo werkt het:
1. Ga naar gemini.google.com (of aistudio.google.com) en kies het beeldmodel (Nano Banana).
2. Plak per cartoon het hele blok tussen de streepjes.
3. Vraag om een vierkant plaatje (1:1). Sla het op als <id>.jpg, bv. c03.jpg.
4. Zet alle plaatjes in één map en zeg het tegen Claude: die maakt er webp van, zet ze in het spel en pusht (= live).

Tip: hang c01.webp en c23.webp (map cartoons) erbij als voorbeeld, dan blijven de helden hetzelfde.

==================== c03 – Bij de apotheek ====================
Make a square 1:1 image. Single-panel comic book illustration in vintage pop-art style: bold black ink outlines, flat colours with Ben-Day halftone dot shading, slightly off-register print on cream paper. Strict limited palette: ultramarine blue, sunny yellow, bubblegum pink, cream white, black. Original characters only, not resembling any existing superhero. Recurring cast: CAPTAIN COASTER, a slightly chubby, overconfident male superhero in an ultramarine suit with a yellow cape, yellow eye mask and a plain round yellow disc on his chest, short dark brown side-parted hair; PINK PANIC, a sharp, unimpressed female superhero in a bubblegum pink suit with a pink eye mask and short black hair; DOCTOR FAKE, a clunky boxy retro robot villain with antenna, round eyes and a pink metal body. Cheeky and suggestive but fully clothed or covered, no nudity. Expressive faces, clear staging, readable at small size. Keep the top third of the image calm and uncluttered (plain sky, plain wall or ceiling of the same scene) so speech bubbles can be added later; do not draw an empty caption box or a second panel, the scene fills the whole frame. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no captions, no logos, no signature. Captain Coaster at a pharmacy counter, nervously sliding a very small plain box across the counter while looking away. The elderly female pharmacist holds up the box and raises one eyebrow. Other customers lean in to look.
